A personal fork of SimpMusic, built on the same open source core

<br><b>What this fork adds:</b>

- Rediscover: tracks you played a lot and haven't touched in months
- You might like: radio seeded from your own recent listening
- Artist mixes: one radio per artist, ranked by your own play count
- Wrapped, monthly recap playlists, and an on-device Analytics tab
- All from a listening history that stays on the device

<br><b>Everything from SimpMusic:</b>

- Play music from YouTube Music or YouTube for free, without ads and in the background
- Three Now Playing styles: Classic, Material 3 Expressive and Apple Music
- Ten-band equalizer with presets and AutoEq headphone profiles, plus Delay and Reverb
- Browsing Home, Charts, Podcasts, Moods & Genres with YouTube Music data at high speed
- Search everything on YouTube
- Spotify Canvas and Animated Album Art supported
- Play 1080p video with subtitles
- AI song suggestions and AI lyrics translation (\*)
- Listen Together: shared rooms that play in sync with friends
- Notifications from followed artists
- Caching and offline playback support
- Synced lyrics from SimpMusic Lyrics, LRCLIB, Spotify (requires login) and YouTube Transcript,
  with romanization for 12 languages
- Multi-YouTube-account support
- Supports SponsorBlock and Return YouTube Dislike
- Sleep Timer
- Android Auto with online content
- And many more!

<br>(\*) Use your own OpenAI or Gemini API key
